The authentication module provides a comprehensive and robust solution for managing user sessions. It leverages industry-standard JWT tokens, ensuring secure and seamless access control across your application.

To get started, developers can utilize the initialization method, which facilitates the establishment of a session context. Additionally, the module offers a myriad of configuration options, enabling teams to tailor the behavior to their specific requirements.

It is important to note that token refresh is handled automatically. The implementation of a background renewal process ensures that users experience uninterrupted access, thereby elevating the overall user experience.
