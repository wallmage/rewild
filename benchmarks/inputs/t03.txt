We are pleased to announce that version 4.2 of our data platform is now generally available. This release marks a pivotal moment in our commitment to delivering a seamless and comprehensive user experience.

Key highlights include: enhanced query performance, delivering up to 40% faster response times; a redesigned permissions model, ensuring granular access control across teams; and native support for Parquet, enabling more efficient storage.

Additionally, we have streamlined the onboarding flow, reducing setup time from approximately 45 minutes to under 10. Observers have cited onboarding friction as a significant barrier to adoption, and we are proud to have addressed it.

We look forward to seeing what our customers build. Exciting times lie ahead.
